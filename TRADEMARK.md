## What the software licence does and does not cover

The licence in `LICENSE` covers the source code. It does not give you any right to use these names or the AT logo. That is true whichever licence the code is under.

## What you may do

- Say, truthfully, that your work is "based on Atelier" or "a fork of Atelier by ArciTech", with a link to the official project.
- Describe compatibility, for example "works with Atelier".
- Use the names plainly to refer to the project in articles, tutorials, reviews and discussion.

## What you may not do

- Name your fork, product, service, company, domain or app-store listing "Atelier", "ArciTech" or anything confusingly similar.
- Use the AT logo, or a logo that looks like it, on a modified or unofficial build.
- Suggest that your build is official, endorsed, certified or supported by ArciTech when it is not.

If you distribute a modified copy, please rename it and remove the AT logo and the ArciTech name from the interface, the title bar and the documents, keeping the copyright and licence notices that the licences require.
